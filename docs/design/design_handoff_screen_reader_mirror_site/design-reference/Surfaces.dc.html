Surface B

Browser extension

For pages behind a sign-in and for apps that change as you use them. The mirror lives in the browser's own side panel, re-runs as the page changes, and can follow keyboard focus.

1

Toolbar icon and popupb1

After install, pin the icon. Its badge shows the flagged count for the current tab. Clicking it opens a small popup: counts, the switches that matter most, and the one button that opens the side panel.
