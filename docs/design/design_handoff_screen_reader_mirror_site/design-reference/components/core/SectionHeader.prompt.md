SectionHeader: the opening block of every section and page. Kicker in the accent, bold tight headline, muted lead capped at 60ch.

```jsx
<SectionHeader kicker="Live demo" title="Try it on a sample page." lead="Three steps. What you see in step 2 is the sample, not this site." />
```

Landing: display → h2s. Inner pages: h1 → h2s. Never more than one lead paragraph.
